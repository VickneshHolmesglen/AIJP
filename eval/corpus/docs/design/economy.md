# Economy

Gold is the only currency. It is earned from bounties, wave income and interest, and spent on
towers and upgrades.

## Starting resources
The player starts each run with 200 gold and 20 lives. The starting gold is enough for three Arrow
Towers or one Cannon Tower plus one Arrow Tower.

## Wave income
When a wave is cleared the player receives a base income of 100 gold plus 10 gold per wave number.
Clearing a wave without leaking any enemy also grants a flawless bonus of 25 gold.

## Interest
At the start of each wave the player earns interest of 5 percent on unspent gold. Interest is
capped at 50 gold per wave so that hoarding never beats building. The cap exists purely to keep
the early game tense.

## Lives
Each leaked enemy costs 1 life. A leaked boss costs 5 lives. When lives reach zero the run ends
and the score screen is shown.
