# Status Effects

Status effects are applied by towers and tick once per second on the affected enemy.

## Poison
Poison deals 4 damage per second per stack. It stacks up to 3 times. Applying Poison again
refreshes the duration to 5 seconds for all stacks. Poison ignores armor, which makes the Venom
Tower the best answer to Brutes and the Shardlord.

## Chill
Chill slows movement by 30 percent for 2 seconds. Chill does not stack; reapplying only refreshes
the timer. Wraiths are immune.

## Burn
Burn deals 6 damage per second for 3 seconds. Burn does not stack. An enemy that is already
burning takes 20 percent extra damage from Arrow Tower bolts, which rewards mixed defences.

## Interaction rules
Poison and Burn can be active on the same enemy at the same time. Chill and Burn cancel each
other: applying Chill removes Burn and applying Burn removes Chill.
