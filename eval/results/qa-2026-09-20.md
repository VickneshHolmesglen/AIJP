Answers the judge marked unsupported:

- `p04` (answerable): If the final villain (the Shardlord) gets past your defences and reaches the core, it is considered a leak. Since the Shardlord is a boss, leaking it costs 5 lives instead of the standard 1 life [2][5].
- `i03` (answerable): SELL_REFUND_RATIO is defined in src/towers/tower.gd (value 0.6) and config/game.yaml (value 0.6).
- `n08` (answerable): It costs 262 gold to upgrade a tower from level 1 to level 3. The calculation is based on the formula in source [2] (base_cost * 0.75 * (level + 1)) with base_cost=50 from source [5]. Upgrading to level 2 costs 50 * 0.75 * 2 = 75. Upgrading to level 3 costs 50 * 0.75 * 3 = 112. Total cost = 75 + 112 = 187? Wait, let's re-read the formula carefully. The formula is `int(base_cost * 0.75 * (level + 1))`. When upgrading TO level N, the cost uses N. So to go from 1 to 2, N=2, cost = 50*0.75*2 = 75. To go from 2 to 3, N=3, cost = 50*0.75*3 = 112. Total = 187. However, source [1] says 'Upgrading to level N costs 0.75 times the base cost times N'. This matches the code. So total cost is 187.
- `c08` (answerable): The enrage behavior for bosses is implemented in the `_enrage` function within `src/enemies/enemy.gd`. This function is called from the `take_damage` method when a boss's health drops below 25% of its maximum health and it is not already enraged. The logic is found at lines 30-31 of `take_damage` (Source [3]) which checks `if health < max_health * 0.25 and is_boss()`, and the behavior itself (increasing speed and emitting a signal) is defined in `_enrage` (Source [1]).
- `u11` (near): Chill lasts for 2 seconds against bosses.
