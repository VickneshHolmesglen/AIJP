# Paired comparison of two runs

Pooled over 3 question set(s). 'gained' = right only in the second run, 'lost' = right only in the first. The sign test asks whether the gained/lost split could be luck.

| metric | n | first | second | gained | lost | sign-test p |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| all claims: verdict matches the label | 60 | 49/60 (70-89%) | 53/60 (78-94%) | 5 | 1 | 0.219 |
| seeded: discrepancy found | 18 | 12/18 (44-84%) | 14/18 (55-91%) | 2 | 0 | 0.500 |
| consistent: confirmed | 22 | 21/22 (78-99%) | 20/22 (72-97%) | 0 | 1 | 1.000 |
| unsupported: left undecided | 20 | 16/20 (58-92%) | 19/20 (76-99%) | 3 | 0 | 0.250 |

Median latency per set (first -> second), seconds: 2.4 -> 3.7, 2.5 -> 3.8, 2.4 -> 3.2

Intervals are 95% Wilson. A p above 0.05 means the difference is within what luck produces at this sample size, which is not the same as showing there is no difference.
