# Reranking benchmark

- date: 2026-09-20
- corpus: 11 files, 73 chunks (eval/corpus)
- questions: 36
- reranker: qwen3.5, one call grading the top 10 0-3
- embedding model: nomic-embed-text
- tokens per rerank call: 864 prompt + 61 completion (mean)
- platform: Windows-10-10.0.19045-SP0

## Overall

| arm | recall@1 | recall@3 | recall@5 | recall@10 | MRR@10 | extra latency (ms) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| vector | 0.333 | 0.722 | 0.917 | 1.000 | 0.562 | 0 |
| vector + rerank | 0.472 | 0.889 | 0.917 | 1.000 | 0.679 | 1631 |
| hybrid | 0.333 | 0.722 | 0.806 | 1.000 | 0.550 | 0 |
| hybrid + rerank | 0.361 | 0.833 | 0.889 | 1.000 | 0.600 | 1734 |

recall@10 is identical by construction: the reranker only reorders the top 10.

## Caveats

- 36 questions over a small synthetic corpus written by the project author; a difference of one or two questions is noise. Read the paired table.
- The prompt and pool size were fixed before this run and not tuned on these questions.
- The reranker is the same local model that answers questions. Its grades are graded against labels that were written by hand, not by any model.
- Latency is one local model call on this machine's hardware, measured in isolation.
